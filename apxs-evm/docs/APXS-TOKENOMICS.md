# 1. Overview

APXS is the native economic asset of the Apraxus network.

Apraxus is designed as an infrastructure layer for autonomous agents, programmable payments, machine-to-machine transactions, network participation, and decentralized economic applications.

APXS is intended to provide the economic foundation for transactions and interactions within the Apraxus ecosystem.

The mainnet token model uses a fixed maximum supply of:

**1,000,000,000 APXS**

No additional APXS issuance is planned under the fixed-supply mainnet model.

The mainnet supply will be established through the genesis allocation and subsequently distributed according to the allocation, vesting, treasury, ecosystem, liquidity, and network incentive mechanisms described in this document.

# 3. Supply Model

APXS follows a fixed maximum supply model.

The maximum possible APXS supply is:

**1,000,000,000 APXS**

No additional issuance is planned after the initial mainnet supply has been established.

The protocol must enforce the maximum supply at the consensus and/or token-contract level where applicable.

The total supply must never exceed:

**1,000,000,000 APXS**

Burning APXS may reduce the effective circulating and total supply, but burned tokens cannot be reissued under the fixed-supply model.

# 17. Fixed-Supply Policy

The intended mainnet model is a fixed maximum supply.

Maximum supply:

**1,000,000,000 APXS**

Under the fixed-supply model:

- No additional APXS issuance is planned.
- Genesis allocation cannot exceed the maximum supply.
- Network incentives must be funded from the predefined allocation.
- Treasury reserves must remain within the predefined allocation.
- Burning may reduce total supply.
- Burned APXS cannot be reissued.

Any future change to the supply model would require an explicit protocol-level decision and corresponding technical, economic, governance, and documentation changes.

---

# 18. Mainnet Genesis

Mainnet genesis will establish the initial APXS economic state.

The genesis process must ensure:

1. Total allocated APXS equals the intended genesis supply.
2. Total supply never exceeds 1,000,000,000 APXS.
3. Every allocation has an explicitly defined destination.
4. Treasury balances are separately identifiable.
5. Team allocations follow the approved vesting structure.
6. Strategic allocations follow the approved vesting structure.
7. Locked allocations cannot accidentally become immediately circulating.
8. TGE circulating supply is limited to the approved 100,000,000 APXS.
9. Genesis balances can be independently verified.
10. Genesis configuration is reproducible and auditable.

Final mainnet genesis addresses will be documented separately before mainnet launch.

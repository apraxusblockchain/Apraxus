## 1. Overview

APXS is the native economic asset of the Apraxus network.

The token is designed to support an infrastructure layer for autonomous agents, programmable payments, machine-to-machine transactions, network participation, and ecosystem development.

The mainnet token model is designed around a fixed maximum supply of:

**1,000,000,000 APXS**

The final mainnet genesis allocation will distribute this supply across protocol development, ecosystem growth, network incentives, liquidity, treasury reserves, strategic development, and the core team.

## 11. Circulating Supply

Maximum supply and circulating supply are separate concepts.

### Maximum Supply

The maximum supply is:

**1,000,000,000 APXS**

### Circulating Supply

Circulating supply represents APXS that is publicly available and not subject to:

- Team vesting
- Strategic vesting
- Treasury control
- Ecosystem lockups
- Contractual restrictions
- Other non-circulating allocations

The official mainnet circulating supply will be determined from the final genesis allocation and actual token release schedule.

No estimated circulating supply should be represented as final until the mainnet genesis configuration is finalized.

## 15. Fixed-Supply Policy

The intended mainnet model is a fixed maximum supply.

The target maximum supply is:

**1,000,000,000 APXS**

No additional token issuance is planned under the fixed-supply model.

Any change to the supply model would require an explicit protocol-level decision and corresponding technical, economic, and documentation changes.

---

## 16. Genesis Allocation

Mainnet genesis will establish the initial APXS allocation.

The genesis process must ensure that:

1. Total allocated APXS does not exceed 1,000,000,000 APXS.
2. Every genesis allocation has an explicitly defined destination.
3. Locked allocations cannot accidentally become immediately circulating.
4. Treasury balances are separately identifiable.
5. Team and strategic allocations follow their vesting schedules.
6. The resulting supply can be independently verified.

The final genesis addresses will be documented separately from this tokenomics document.
